openlaunch connects agents to device capabilities you approve. Start with a health check, an LED, or a message on an Uno's matrix. The device stays independent of your AI provider.

A **device** is your board. An **agent** is the software asking it to do something. A **capability** is one specific operation, such as setting an LED. A **grant** is your permission for one agent to use capabilities on one device until an expiry time.

## Your first connection

1. [Start your console](/setup).
2. Pair an [Uno R4 WiFi](/uno-r4) or [Pi 4](/pi).
3. [Connect an agent](/agents) and approve health access.
4. Ask for a health check, then inspect its completed result.
